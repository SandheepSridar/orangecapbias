Best playing XI — who gives us the best chance of winning?

Ranked by a batting value score (average + strike rate) and a bowling value score (economy + dot-ball rate), both measured against the whole league, not just our own squad — so this reflects real quality, not just "best of our bunch." The wicketkeeper is set from scorecard credit, then we fill 5 more batting spots, enough bowling options to cover a full attack, and the best value left over. Min 5 innings batted / 8 overs bowled to qualify — small-sample cameos are excluded.

Click a player below to mark them unavailable for this match — the XI reshuffles instantly from the rest of the squad.

Who do we depend on to win? — not just batting

For every player with enough matches, we split their games into their own "good" half and "bad" half — runs for batters, economy for bowlers — and compare our win rate in each. The gap ("swing") is how much our result seems to move with that player specifically. Small season, so read this as a notable pattern, not a guarantee.
