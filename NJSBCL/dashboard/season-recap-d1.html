MOVI (Middle-Order Value Index) rewards the batters the run-tally award overlooks: the ones who bat at No. 4 to 7 and get fewer balls to face. The Gladiators won their first four matches, lost to Jersey Boys on 17 May after making 74 for 8, and then won eight in a row from 30 May to 25 July. That run included a 72-run win over Nj Tigers, held to 43 for 9 while Vinit Bharadwaj took 5 for 14, and the season's highest total, 146 for 9 against F5.

August brought two of the three league defeats. Piscataway Avengers bowled the Gladiators out for 80 chasing 110, and SAMP XI won by 3 wickets after the Gladiators were bowled out for 63. In between came a 4-run defence against Gryffindors, and a 32-run win over Blue Aces CC closed the league stage. The Gladiators finished fourth in Group A, level on 78 points with third-placed Warriors and behind them on net run rate.

In the knockouts the bowlers carried a batting order that made 79 all out and 71 all out in the first two rounds. Storm Riders were held to 71, with Ankur Chaturvedi bowling four overs for 9 runs and 20 dot balls. Then came Pway Lions, the Group A top seed, who had lost one league match all season. Naveen R Sripathi's 5 for 6 left them 69 for 9, 2 runs short. In the semi-final Harshad Patil's 46 off 26 balls took the Gladiators to 130 for 7, and Newport CC were bowled out for 76.

Final · 24 Oct 2026

52 all out, and the title

Piscataway Avengers, who had beaten the Gladiators by 29 runs on 2 August, won the toss and batted. They were bowled out in 15.2 overs, and only Niraj Patel (23 off 15 balls) reached double figures. Naveen R Sripathi bowled four overs for 5 runs with 21 dot balls, and Ankur Chaturvedi four overs for 9. Harshad Patil and Vinit Bharadwaj took three wickets each, and Vinit finished it with wickets off consecutive balls. The chase took 8.3 overs: Dileep Reddy made 27 off 25 balls, and the Gladiators reached 58 for 3 to win by 7 wickets.
