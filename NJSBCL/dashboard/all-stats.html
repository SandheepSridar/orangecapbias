More accurate than cricclubs' stats pages

Every number here is added up from the individual match scorecards. cricclubs' own season stats pages get three of our bowlers wrong, so their totals won't match ours. The scorecards show that ours are correct:

  • Vinit Bharadwaj and Sandeep A Shetty: both bowled two separate spells in the Division 1 match against F5. cricclubs only counts one spell for each, so it misses 3 overs, 13 runs and 2 wickets for Vinit, and 2 overs, 18 runs and 1 wicket for Sandeep.
  • Suraj Pai Madhukar: cricclubs counts every one of his matches twice, in both series, so his batting and bowling figures there are double the real ones. It even lists more innings than matches played.

Every other bowler checked matches cricclubs exactly.
