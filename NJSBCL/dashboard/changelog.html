Changelog

What's been added to the Scout dashboard, most recent first.

Sun, Sep 20 2026

  • Opponent dropdown is knockout-only — now the group stage is done, the dropdown lists just the 15 teams we can still actually meet (our group's other seven qualifiers plus the other group's eight) instead of everyone we played on the way here, and it opens on our confirmed pre-quarter opponent rather than whoever came first alphabetically.
  • Knockouts page — a new page for the bracket now the group stage is done: our pre-quarter tie with an Elo win probability and a quick scouting read, the full eight-tie draw with our match highlighted, who qualified from each group (and who just missed the cut), and what the rule book actually says — qualification under rule 7, the four rounds, the seeding tiebreaks, and the full Super Over chain that decides a level knockout match.
  • Direct links into a matchup — the Knockouts page links straight to the full scouting page for our opponent, with the right series and team already selected.
  • Knockout opponents in the dropdown — you can now scout teams we never meet in the league stage. Both series run a cross-group league (we only ever play the other group), so a knockout rival used to be unpickable: Division 1 now lists every Group A team, and the Weekenders Cup lists every Group A team plus Group B's top 8 — the full field either of us can draw in the playoffs.
  • Weekenders Group B now scouted — Group B's top 8 had no match data on the site at all, so their pages would have come up empty. Every one of their matches this season is now scraped, with full batting, bowling and over-by-over detail behind the usual sections.

Sun, Aug 30 2026

  • Fairer recap after a loss — the Last match recap no longer reads as a post-mortem when we lose: verdicts are framed neutrally, and each one now shows the impact breakdown behind it so you can see why it was scored right or wrong.

Wed, Aug 26 2026

  • NRR scenario tree — on the Standings page, every combination of results in our remaining fixtures, showing the best and worst league position each one leaves us in, with net run rate projected forward rather than guessed.
  • Standings page — the full group tables, both groups, in one place, instead of only the single opponent's line shown on the matchup page.

Sat, Aug 22 2026

  • Recap verdict fixes — key-batsman verdicts were firing off an inverted win-rate split (calling a read right when it had actually gone the other way), bowling verdicts mis-scored an exact boundary tie, and a bowler's figures across two separate spells weren't being combined. All three now score the way the Methodology page describes.

Tue, Aug 18 2026

  • Page-view counter — the nav now shows how many times the dashboard has been viewed over the last 2 weeks, replacing the feedback email banner and staying visible on mobile.

Sun, Aug 16 2026

  • Collapsible sections on mobile — sections start collapsed on a phone so you can see what's on the page at a glance and open just the one you want.
  • Mobile layout fixes — the nav no longer overflows the screen, the sticky filter bar sits at the right offset, and wide tables scroll sideways instead of stretching the page.
  • Top Gladiators ticker shows 3 — the ticker now lists the top 3 by season impact score in each series, up from 2.
  • Top Gladiators ticker — a horizontal auto-scrolling strip below the feedback banner (every page except Methodology/Changelog) showing the top 2 Gladiators by season-total impact score in each series — the same z-scored Star of the Match formula, totalled across every completed match so far.
  • Pinned filter bar — the Series/Opponent/Matchup controls now stay visible at the top of the page while scrolling instead of scrolling out of view.

Sat, Aug 15 2026

  • Smarter opponent auto-select — the opponent dropdown now defaults to whoever we play next on the schedule (not just alphabetically first), and a completed match immediately drops off "Upcoming fixtures" instead of lingering until the next calendar day.
  • AI insight follow-through — the recap page now checks whether each season AI insight was actually acted on in the latest match and what happened, including honest "right process, bad individual outcome" calls — plus a broader check for any opposition bowler quietly running below their season economy, not just the 3 explicitly named weak bowlers.
  • Feedback banner — a persistent "spotted something broken?" email link at the top of every page.
  • Last match recap page — a standalone page with what-we-got-right/wrong verdicts (toss read, par score, key-batsman threshold, bowling strengths/weaknesses) and a Star of the Match pick, scored fairly: batting and bowling points are each z-scored against the whole league before combining, fixing a bias where a routine bowling spell used to outscore a good batting innings.

Fri, Aug 14 2026

  • "How this is calculated" links — most dashboard sections now link straight to their exact explanation on the Methodology page.
  • Methodology page added — explains the formula behind every number on the site, from Elo win probability to z-scored comparisons.
  • AI insights — top 3 data-backed suggestions worth reconsidering (bowling changes, batting order), auto-generated by comparing season stats against what's actually happening on the field.

Thu, Aug 13 2026

  • Key-batsman win impact & win dependency — shows how much our win rate swings with a specific top batsman getting out early, plus which players (batting or bowling) our results lean on most.
  • "On this page" navigation — a jump-to-section sidebar on the matchup page for wide screens.
  • Opponent recent form on fixture cards — W/L/T dots showing each upcoming opponent's last 5 results league-wide (not just head-to-head vs us), with a hover tooltip for each result.

Wed, Aug 12 2026

  • Chase-pacing calculator — enter a chase target on the matchup page and it's split into four 4-over blocks, weighted by the opponent's bowling strength.
  • Squad roster added to the charts page, plus fixes to the playing-XI selection logic.
  • Best playing XI picker — interactive: toggle a player's availability and the suggested XI recalculates live in the browser.
  • New "Gladiators charts" page — Elo rating history, full squad batting/bowling leaderboards, other season metrics.
  • Batting collapse detection — flags how often an innings falls apart (3+ of the top 7 batsmen out for a combined ≤20 runs).
  • Upcoming fixtures expanded — added a win-probability badge per fixture and a "show all upcoming matches" toggle.
  • Home/away splits — win% at home vs away per team.

Tue, Aug 11 2026

  • Per-batsman recent form — hot/cold/steady trend badges based on last-5-innings form vs season average.
  • Weak-bowler feature fixes.
  • Bowler-matchup labels — "Our top bowlers" vs "Bowlers to target for runs" column headers.
  • Mobile-friendly layout fixes.
  • Dashboard built from scratch — scraped Division 1 + Weekenders Cup data (scorecards, points tables, schedules) and built the initial site.
