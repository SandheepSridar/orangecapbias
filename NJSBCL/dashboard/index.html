Win probability — is there a way to estimate this?

Elo rating (same idea chess uses): every team starts at 1500 and every result this season nudges both teams' ratings — beating a strong team gains more than beating a weak one. The probability below is just the standard Elo win-expectancy formula applied to each team's rating right now. It's a form/strength estimate, not a crystal ball — it doesn't know about toss, weather, or who's available to play.
