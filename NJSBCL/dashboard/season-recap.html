Gladiators awards

MOVI award

Harshad Patil

average batting position 5.1 · 14 innings

MOVI (Middle-Order Value Index) rewards the batters the run-tally award overlooks: the ones who bat at No. 4 to 7 and get fewer balls to face. Harshad scored at a strike rate of 157 (157 runs per 100 balls), the fastest of any Gladiators regular. His 72 not out off 21 balls, with 11 sixes, batting at No. 5 against Cricket Warriors, was the innings of the season.

Clutch player of the season

Vinit Bharadwaj

top performer in 3 of 6 pressure wins

When a win was in doubt, Vinit decided it with bat and ball. Against Vikings CC he top-scored with 38 off 31 balls in a total of 73, then took 3 wickets for 2 runs in 3 overs as Vikings CC were bowled out for 28. Against Morris Indians he made 27 off 15 balls in a total of 111, then took 2 wickets for 11 runs as the Gladiators won by 17 runs. Against Jc Mavericks he top-scored with 31 off 20 balls in a total of 89, then bowled four overs for 13 as the Gladiators won by 6 runs.

Knockout match. Three league fixtures were no-results and are not shown.

The Gladiators opened with two comfortable wins, lost to MCC despite scoring 100, and then won eight in a row from mid-May to late July. That run included the season's biggest result: 211 for 2 against TRIDENTS XI and a 144-run win, built on Baseer Ahmed Mohammed's 73 off 40 balls and Dileep Reddy's 68 not out off 22 balls.

August was the wobble, with back-to-back defeats to BLOODLINE and NJ Titans. The team answered with four wins in a row, including two tight defences (by 17 against Morris Indians, by 6 against Jc Mavericks) and a 4-wicket chase against Group D's top seed Shreeji XI. That secured first place by three points over Mag11.

The knockouts started at full speed. Express Rangers were bowled out for 46 and the Gladiators chased it down in 29 balls. Karma XI made 78 for 8 and the Gladiators chased it with 44 balls to spare. Then came the quarter-final.

Quarter-final · 18 Oct 2026

47 all out, the season's lowest total

JC Warriors won the toss and bowled. The Gladiators were bowled out in 15.2 overs. Sandeep A Shetty's 14 off 18 balls was the top score, and nobody else made more than 5. The bowlers took 3 wickets defending 47, with Ankur Chaturvedi conceding 7 from 3 overs with 15 dot balls, but JC Warriors reached 48 for 3 in 8.2 overs. All four defeats this season had the same shape: the top three batters scored fewer than 40 runs between them.
