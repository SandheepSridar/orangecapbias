The Middle-Order Value Index

IPL run charts crown whoever accumulates the most — almost always an opener. MOVI asks a different question: who was the best middle-order batsman each season?

An award you cannot win from number five

Batting at No. 5 means arriving mid-innings, with the field spread, the ball soft and ten overs already gone. You can bat brilliantly all season and still finish nowhere on the run charts — there simply aren't enough balls left.

MOVI rates every qualifying middle-order batsman (average position 4–7) on the things raw run tallies ignore, and blends them into one score that is comparable within each season. A higher MOVI means a batsman stood further above his middle-order peers that year.

Four skills, one score

📦

Volume

Runs per innings — how much he delivers each time he walks in.

⚡

Efficiency

Strike rate — how fast those runs come.

🎯

Finishing

Scoring speed in the death overs (16–20), the hardest phase to bat in.

🔁

Consistency

Reliability across the season, not one freak knock.

Each skill is measured against the season's other middle-order batsmen, then the four are blended into a single score.
